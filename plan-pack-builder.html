Plan Pack Builder

Turn your plan prices and limits into one file employees open on their own computer. The file carries your plans and nothing else. Employees type in their own details, and those details never leave their device.

This builder also runs offline. Your plan data stays on this computer.

Step 2

Plan data

One row per plan, as CSV or JSON. Prices are what the employee pays per paycheck at each coverage level. Start from the template if you are not sure of the layout.

CSV layout

The first row is a header. A kind column says medical, dental or vision. Other columns use dotted names, for example premium.fam or deductible.ind. Leave a cell empty if it does not apply. Yes, true or 1 mark a high-deductible plan in the hdhp column. Percentages are whole numbers, such as 80. The template shows every column. Prescription coinsurance with a minimum and maximum uses rxCo.generic.pct, rxCo.generic.min and rxCo.generic.max (and the same for preferred, nonpreferred and specialty). To leave a dental or vision plan out of the comparison until you have its coverage details, put yes in incomplete and write the questions for HR in askHR, separated by a | character. Last year’s prices go in prior.ee, prior.eeSp, prior.eeCh and prior.fam.

Step 2b

Drug list (optional)

Lets employees search for their medications. One row per drug with the columns drug,tier,price,excluded,note. Tier is generic, preferred, nonpreferred or specialty. Price is the usual retail price for a 30-day fill. Put yes in excluded for a drug the plans do not cover. A drug that is not on the list sends the employee to HR.

Step 4

Build the employee file

Plan file ID

Publish this ID next to the file on your intranet or in the enrollment email. The file shows the same ID inside it, so employees can tell they have the genuine copy.

  • Open the file once yourself and try a household or two before you send it.
  • Share it by email, your HR portal or your intranet. One file works for every employee.
  • Make a new file each plan year, and whenever prices or limits change.
